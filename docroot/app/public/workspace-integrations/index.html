Leo Pitt Workspace Integrations

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Leo Pitt Workspace Integrations connects Google accounts authorised by Leo Pitt to his productivity tools.

The integration supports working with Gmail, calendars, Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, contacts and Chat, according to the permissions granted by each account. It can retrieve and summarise information and perform requested actions, including sending messages and creating, updating or deleting supported items.

Separate account permissions

Each Google account signs in and grants consent separately. Owning the integration does not grant access to other accounts. Access can be revoked independently for each account.

Private use

This integration is operated by Leo Pitt for his own authorised personal and work accounts. It is not a public sign-up service. These pages provide information about the integration; visiting them does not connect a Google account.

Data handling

Information needed for a requested task may be sent to connected productivity services. Read the privacy policy for details about access, storage, sharing and deletion, and the terms of use.
