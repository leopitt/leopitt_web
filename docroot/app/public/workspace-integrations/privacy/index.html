Leo Pitt Workspace Integrations

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy describes how Leo Pitt Workspace Integrations, operated by Leo Pitt, handles information from individually authorised Google accounts.

Information accessed and purpose

Depending on the permissions granted, the integration can access account identity, email messages and attachments, calendars and events, Drive files and documents, spreadsheets, presentations, forms, task lists, contacts and Chat messages. It uses this information to carry out tasks requested through connected tools, such as finding information, summarising content, coordinating schedules and creating or changing Google items.

Permissions can include sending email and creating, updating or deleting items. Each account grants permission separately through Google OAuth. Google passwords are not collected by the integration.

Connected tools and sharing

When used through Codex, information returned by the integration is provided to OpenAI to process the requested task and may appear in the associated chat. When used through OpenClaw, information may be provided to the model or service configured for that workflow. The provider and account settings for a workflow must be reviewed before Google data is sent to it. Additional services require an appropriate disclosure and authorisation before use.

Data is not sold or used by this integration for advertising. Transfers are limited to providing the authorised functionality, security needs or legal obligations. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

The local Workspace MCP setup stores OAuth credentials and per-account access tokens on the computer running it, with separate account credential folders. Retrieved information and generated outputs can persist in the connected service's chat history, local files, logs or backups, according to the workflow and that service's settings. There is no single automatic deletion period covering these separate systems.

Credentials are retained while the connection is in use. Information saved to complete a task remains until it is deleted from the relevant device or service, subject to any applicable backup, provider or legal retention requirements. The public information pages do not receive OAuth tokens or Google account content.

Revocation and deletion

Remove the integration's access in Google Account connections to stop future authorised access for that account. Revocation does not delete information already saved elsewhere. Delete local token files and saved outputs on the relevant device, and use the connected service's controls to delete its chats or files. Contact Leo Pitt for help locating or deleting integration-managed copies.

Security and changes

OAuth credentials should be kept private, stored with restricted device permissions and excluded from public repositories. Account holders should only connect accounts and services they are authorised to use. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and, where required, require renewed consent before the new use.
